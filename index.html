Hierarchical Modeling: Claw Arm

Chain: base → lower arm → upper arm → hand → two fingers. Move a joint and every part after it follows.
Touch a prop with the tip of the claw and it sticks like a magnet. Lift it, swivel, then press Reset to drop it.
Drag the canvas to orbit. Arrow keys and w, s move the camera. Press F12 to see the console for debugging.
Edit the vertex and fragment shaders on the left to get the result instantly.
